# Eyebrow

The content slide header: a mono eyebrow, the slide title, and an optional subtitle. Every content slide uses it at the same position.

- Provide: the eyebrow as `NN · LABEL` (section number, middle dot, uppercase label), the title in sentence case, and optionally one subtitle line.
- Position: top-left at `slide-pad-top` and `slide-pad-x`; the logo sits top-right on the same line as the eyebrow.
- Do keep the title on one line. Don't end a single-sentence title with a full stop; don't use the eyebrow for anything but the section.
