# SectionDivider

The slide that opens a section: `navy` ground, the section number in mono teal at 180px, the section title at 132px in white. Shown here at 40% and cropped to half height.

- Provide: the two-digit section number and a one or two word title.
- The numbers match the eyebrows of the content slides that follow (`04 · COMPOSITIONS`).
- Same in both deck themes: divider slides are always navy.
